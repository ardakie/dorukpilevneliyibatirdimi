OVERVIEW

Art Basel Hong Kong takes place from March 27–29, 2026, at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. Recognized as one of the most influential contemporary art fairs in Asia, the event brings together the region’s dynamic cultural landscape with leading international galleries.

 

This year, PİLEVNELİ will present a curated selection at Booth 3C27, offering a focused presentation that reflects the gallery’s ongoing engagement with contemporary artistic practices. The booth brings together works across various disciplines through diverse material and conceptual approaches.

 

Set within the vibrant context of Hong Kong’s contemporary art scene, Art Basel Hong Kong continues to present a compelling overview of global artistic production. With its ambitious gallery presentations, large-scale installations, conversations, and public programs, the fair offers a multifaceted platform for engaging with contemporary art.

 

For visiting hours and ticket information, please visit the official Art Basel Hong Kong website.

 

Address:
Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre
1 Expo Drive, Wan Chai, Hong Kong
