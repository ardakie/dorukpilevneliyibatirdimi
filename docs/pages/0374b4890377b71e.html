Event: TAIPEI DANGDAI ART & IDEAS: 6TH EDITION | PİLEVNELİ

OVERVIEW

The 6th edition of Taipei Dangdai Art & Ideas is taking place from May 9–11, 2025, at the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center. Now recognized as one of Asia’s leading contemporary art events, the fair brings together Taipei’s vibrant creative energy with the global art scene.

 

This year, PİLEVNELİ will present a curated selection at Booth B08, featuring works by :mentalKLINIK, Bora Akıncıtürk, Rasim Aksan, Refik Anadol, Banu Anka, Lal Batman, Ali Elmacı, Serkan Sarıer, and Tarık Töre. The participating artists, working across various disciplines, offer fresh interpretations of concepts such as space, the body, memory, and digital consciousness.

 

Set against the backdrop of Taiwan’s dynamic art scene, this year’s fair showcases some of the most compelling examples of contemporary global art. With its curatorial programs, site-specific installations, artist talks, and guided tours, Taipei Dangdai offers a rich and diverse platform that engages deeply with the cultural fabric of Taipei.


For visiting hours and ticket information, please visit the official Taipei Dangdai website.

Address:

No. 1, Jingmao 2nd Road, Nangang District, Taipei City
