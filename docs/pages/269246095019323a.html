Arik Levy’s “SilentFutures” Exhibition Opens at Museum DKM | PİLEVNELİ

Arik Levy’s exhibition SilentFutures will be on view at Museum DKM from March 29, 2025, to January 31, 2026. Located in the center of Duisburg, a city in the Düsseldorf region of Germany, the museum’s minimalist architecture provides a focused setting for experiencing the artworks.

 

The exhibition highlights Levy’s interdisciplinary practice, particularly his innovative approach to sculpture, installation, and design. The artist summarizes his creative philosophy with the statement: “Creation is a muscle without control.”

 

Embracing a multidisciplinary approach, Levy works across a wide range of fields—from design to stage sets, from sculptures to wearable technologies. Known for his public sculptures and site-specific works, Levy emphasizes that it is not objects but people who give meaning to the world. SilentFutures offers a valuable opportunity to explore his artistic vision and unique relationship with space.
