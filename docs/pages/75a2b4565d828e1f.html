The sales of a limited number of NFTs created by Refik Anadol using the data of the "Inspiration4" space mission have been concluded. The entire collection sold for $6.2 million. US$2.5 million in sales revenue of the NFT collection, aligning with the purpose of the “Inspiration4” mission, goes to St. Jude's Children's Research Hospital. SpaceX space mission named “Inspiration4” is located in St. It took place in 2021 to raise funds for Jude's Children's and Research Hospital.

 

“Inspiration4” was the first civilian manned space flight in history, with no professional astronauts in its crew, completing a 3-day journey around the Earth's orbit. In order to raise awareness and raise funds, billionaire Jared Isaacman secured $100 million of the donation himself and donated a place for 3 people to join him in the space flight he financed.
