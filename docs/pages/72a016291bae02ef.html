Event: ART DUSSELDORF: 7TH EDITION | PİLEVNELİ

PİLEVNELİ at Art Düsseldorf 2025

 

Art Düsseldorf, a contemporary art fair with strong ties to Germany’s rich artistic heritage and a distinguished curatorial program, is set to welcome the art world from April 10–13, 2025, at Areal Böhler.

This year, PİLEVNELİ will present the recent works of :mentalKLINIK, Lal Batman, and Serkan Sarıer at Art Düsseldorf. Showcased at Booth G12, the selection highlights themes such as the invisible political dynamics of coexistence, the sensory complexity of belonging, and fluid identities.

 

Bringing together international art professionals, collectors, and enthusiasts each year, Art Düsseldorf continues to offer a comprehensive program. The fair serves as a dynamic platform for discovering and discussing contemporary art practices, directly linked to Düsseldorf’s rich cultural legacy.

With over 90 galleries and more than 40,000 visitors annually, Art Düsseldorf continues to expand. Half of the exhibited works come from Germany, while the other half represent artists from around the world. Held in Areal Böhler, a venue known for its industrial architecture and strong connection to art, the fair provides a distinctive setting that embraces various disciplines of contemporary art.

To check visiting hours and ticket details, please visit the official Art Düsseldorf website.
