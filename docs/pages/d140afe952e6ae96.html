PILEVNELI is pleased to announce the representation of RASİM AKSAN | PİLEVNELİ

PİLEVNELİ is pleased to announce the representation of Rasim Aksan! Born in 1984 in Osmancık, Rasim Aksan explores the depths of human relationships, space, and visual perception through a figurative and hyper-realistic style. By blending classical references with contemporary themes, Aksan confronts the viewer with a layered visual world where the past and present intertwine. He invites the audience to move beyond being passive observers. Combining acrylic, calligraphy ink, and marbling paint with airbrush techniques, he creates highly sophisticated and detailed compositions regardless of scale.

The artist is preparing to present his first solo exhibition and monograph under the PİLEVNELİ Gallery in September 2025.
