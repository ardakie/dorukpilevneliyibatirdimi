BJARNE MELGAARD'S FIRST NFT PROJECT “THE LIGHTBULB MAN” IS OUT! | PİLEVNELİ

/news/10-bjarne-melgaard-s-first-nft-project-the-lightbulb-man/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2025-06-25
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.

One of the artist of PILEVNELI Bjarne Melgaard’s first NFT project “The Lightbulb Man” is out!

 

Starting from the series produced by Melgaard in 1998, the project consists of 1122 unique works.

 

NFT lovers, who do not know which artifact they bought during the purchase, will learn which artifact they bought on January 24. In addition, every collector who buys NFT from the series will have the right to give the work any name they want. Thus, Melgaard's collectors will be permanently etched on the Ethereum Blockchain with the artifact.

 

 

Bjarne Melgaard's Lightbulb Man series is available at www.lightbulbman.com.
