PİLEVNELİ at Art Show: Galleries Meeting!

The exhibition, which will be held with the main sponsorship of Bilgili Holding, which makes valuable contributions to the cultural and artistic life of Istanbul, and the corporate sponsorship of Yapı Kredi Private Banking, will jointly undertake the architectural design in order to bring together different galleries under a common language, while TBWA will provide communication and corporate identity support. AVANT D’ART will handle the video content development strategy and documentation, and Yöntem Communications will handle public relations.
 
The event is organized with the advertising and promotion support of İBB Kültür AŞ and Şişli Municipality. Jaguar, which Borusan Otomotiv is the distributor of in Turkey, will be the transportation sponsor of the event. The Art Talks of the event will be supported by Tosyalı Holding. iyzico, Knauf, Damat Tween and Nart Sigorta ve Reasürans Brokerliği, as well as Vestel, Kalebodur, Jotun, Zuhal Müzik, Atelier Rebul, Yunus Karma, Mozaik, Mudo Concept, House of Beadzz, Duran Doğan Packaging, ABC Deterjan, Elta360 x ArtPick, Republika Aparts, TOST, 400°C Pizza, Bakırkazan, Uludağ and Prosense Güvenlik are providing support in various areas. The media sponsorship of the event is undertaken by Aposto, ArtDog, ARTtv, Binat Architecture Media Group, Canvas Magazine, Eventmag, Gentleman, Hello!, ICON dooh, L'Officiel Türkiye, Maison Française, Marketing Türkiye, Oggusto, Oksijen, Panoffect, Strolling Istanbul, Şalom Gazetesi, XXI Mimarlık and YUZU. In addition, visitors will be able to benefit from various services provided by Minoa, Momo and The Whirl Roastery, which provide support throughout the event.
