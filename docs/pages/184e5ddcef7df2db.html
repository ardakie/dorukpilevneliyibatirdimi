Mehmet & Kazım are in the group exhibition “Seeing the Unseen” at the ERES Foundation in Munich! | PİLEVNELİ

Mehmet & Kazım are participating in the group exhibition “Seeing the Unseen” at the ERES Foundation in Munich with their works Superposition V01, V02, and V03.

 

With “Seeing the Unseen”, the ERES Foundation invites visitors on an interdisciplinary journey into the quantum world. A century after the groundbreaking achievements of Niels Bohr, Werner Heisenberg, and others, the latest scientific findings come together with contemporary art.

 

The exhibition, featuring artists such as Dara Birnbaum, Herbert W. Franke, Elsa Garmire, Ayoung Kim, Alicja Kwade, Roman Lipski, Jonas Lund, Mehmet & Kazım, Tan Mu, Semiconductor, Thomas Struth, Paul Talman, Tamiko Thiel, Troika, Agustina Woodgate, and many more, can be visited until 26 September 2026.

 

Superposition V01, 30 × 40 cm, watercolor on 450 g watercolor paper
Superposition V02, 195 × 270 cm, pencil and watercolor on canvas, 2025
Superposition V03, 4K video, 1:33 min, loop
