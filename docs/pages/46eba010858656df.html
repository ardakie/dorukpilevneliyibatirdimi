PILEVNELI is pleased to announce the representation of Sucuk & Bratwurst! | PİLEVNELİ

The artist collective and creative studio Sucuk & Bratwurst, founded in 2014 by Denis Olgac, Lukas Olgac, Alessandro Belliero and David Gönner, consists mainly of moving and stable three-dimensional and digital works. The four, who are childhood friends, began their creative work together with the blog they established in 2012. The photographs, collages, traditional drawings and three-dimensional works they shared in their blog began to draw attention and Sucuk & Bratwurst began to receive invitations to individual and group expositions. With the establishment of the creative agency in 2014, they began to do design and commercial works. The commercial projects they executed include Alexander Wang, Rimowa, Moncler, Nike, Adidas, Porche, Converse, Dior, and Converse. Sucuk & Bratwurst has also worked with established artists such as Lena Meyer-Landrut, Post Malone and Dua Lipa.
