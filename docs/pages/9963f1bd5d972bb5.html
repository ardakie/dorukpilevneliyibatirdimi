ALİ ELMACI, KISS MY LIPS DAGGER MY HEART | 14 January - 11 March 2023 - Overview | PİLEVNELİ

VIEW WORKS
PILEVNELI is getting ready to host Ali Elmacı's first solo exhibition.
 
Born on October 22, 1976 in Sinop, Ali Elmacı graduated from Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University Painting Department in 2010. The artist, who deals with the criticism of power, media, social patterns and popular culture in his works, interprets both social and personal phenomena in striking dimensions and caricatures the patterns.
 
In his exhibition titled “Kiss My Lips Dagger My Heart”, Elmacı presents to the audience 3 new short-length art films directed by the artist and produced by Bulut Reyhanoğlu, in addition to his latest paper and canvas works. Elmacı's works compiled from private collections support the setup of the exhibition.
 
“Kiss My Lips Dagger My Heart” can be visited at PILEVNELI Dolapdere between January 14 and 11 March, 2023.
