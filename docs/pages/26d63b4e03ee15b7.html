PILEVNELI

Artweeks @ Akaretler

No: 37 - 39 | 1st floor

30.03 - 10.04.2022

 

 

PILEVNELI attends Artweeks @ Akaretler, which will take place between 30 March - 10 April 2022, with a selection of works by seventeen artists.

 

 

PILEVNELI, which will be located on the first floor of no:37-39 of the Akaretler Rowhouses will exhibit works by Bora Akıncıtürk, Nevin Aladağ, Refik Anadol, Nobuyoshi Araki, Johan Creten, Ali Elmacı, Esra Gülmen, Daniel Knorr, Arik Levy, Mehmet & Kazım, Bjarne Melgaard, Serkan Sarıer, Meltem Sarıkaya, Defne Tesal, Tarık Töre, Richard Wilson and Erdoğan Zümrütoğlu.

 

 

 

In collaboration with Mozaik, there will also be designer furniture supporting the works presented at PILEVNELI. Mozaik, the Turkish representative of veteran brands of the decoration arena such as B&B Italia, Cassina, Vitra and Knoll, is celebrating its 30th anniversary this year.

 

 

Organized by Bilgili Holding and Sabiha Kurtulmuş, Artweeks @ Akaretler will take place in buildings 11, 15, 17, 19, 37-39, 55 and B 2/2-3-4 of Akaretler Sıraevler. You can find the 6th edition of Artweeks @ Akaretler special supplement in IstanbulArtNews March issue.
