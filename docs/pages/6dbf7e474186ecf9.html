Mehmet&Kazım are designing the set for Emre Akal’s play “Barbie ein Puppenheim.” | PİLEVNELİ

This version reimagines Henrik Ibsen’s “Nora. A Doll’s House” in the world of Barbie and Ken, questioning the idea of a perfect world. Is it really possible for a society to exist without problems, where everyone lives in harmony? Mehmet & Kazım’s visual style adds a new layer of reality to the play.

The next performances are on October 19, 21, and November 6, 7, and 23 at Thalia Gauß.

PİLEVNELİ congratulates Mehmet&Kazım on their work.
