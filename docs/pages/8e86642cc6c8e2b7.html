PILEVNELI IS HAPPY TO ANNOUNCE NEVİN ALADAĞ'S REPRESENTATION! | PİLEVNELİ

Nevin Aladağ, born in Van in 1972, completed her education at the Munich Fine Arts Academy. Continuing her work in various fields such as sculpture, video, performance, photography and site-specific installation, Aladağ frequently draws on her experiences growing up in Germany in her works. While producing the works of the artist, he searches for new languages, associations and displacements through the subject he deals with, the techniques he uses, and his life experiences. It deforms social, social and psychological boundaries with the help of metaphors.
