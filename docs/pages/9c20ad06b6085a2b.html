HANS OP DE BEECK IS AT THE 16TH LYON BIENNAL! | PİLEVNELİ

/news/21/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2026-06-15
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.

Hans Op De Beeck, one of the artists from PİLEVNELİ, is at the 16th Lyon Biennale! Multi-disciplinary artist Op de Beeck, known for his monochrome sculptures and installations, will take part in the 16th Lyon Biennial, whose theme is "Manifesto of Fragility". Sam Bardaouil and Till Fellrath are the curators of the biennial, which brings together 87 artists.

 

The 16th Lyon Biennial can be visited between 14 September - 31 December 2022 at the macLYON Art Museum, the old Fagor Factory and various venues in the city.
