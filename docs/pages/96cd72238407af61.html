MEHMET & KAZIM'S "NO ARTISTAS NFT COLLECTION" DROP ON MARCH 19,2022 AT OPENSEA | PİLEVNELİ

Artist duo Mehmet & Kazım's "No Artistas NFT Collection" drop will be seen by NFT lovers on March 19, 2022 at Opensea.

 

Each of the 100 different NFT figures have their own unique movements, physical characteristics and sounds. A Whitelist has been created for the figures that will be sold for 0.5 ETH each, which can be registered via Discord. Those who enter the Whitelist are also given a signed print gift of NFTs and a giveaway draw. A virtual exhibition of 100 NFTs was also created in Decentraland by the artist duo.

 

The characters from the world created by Mehmet & Kazım can be accessed from Opensea on March 19, 2022 at 20:00 Turkish time.

 

Opensea: https://opensea.io/collection/no-artistas

 

For the whitelist: https://discord.gg/xJ2JNP2R

 

For Decentraland: https://play.decentraland.org/?position=-56,100
