HÜSEYİN ÇAĞLAYAN, SOUFFLEUR | PİLEVNELİ

Hüseyin Çağlayan's exhibition Souffleur focuses on the body and modern anthropology through new media.
 
Presenting three series of works and a video installation, the exhibition is at Sakıp Sabancı Museum in collaboration with Pilevneli Gallery until 8 January 2023.
September 23, 2022
