MEHMET & KAZIM

MURAL 3000

15.06 - 08.09

PİLEVNELİ | YALIKAVAK

 

TAV Passport sponsors the original mural artwork called Mural 3000 by Mehmet & Kazım located at PİLEVNELİ Yalıkavak. In Mural 3000, Mehmet & Kazım magnifies the characteristic Edding drawings impressively which are reminiscent of "throw-ups," a graffiti style that has shaped metropolises for over 50 years. Inspired by Francisco Goya's Pinturas Negras, abstract expressionists from the 1950s, Paul Klee's Geschwister, Pierre Soulages' works, and modern scenes from Brooklyn's Bushwick, Mehmet & Kazım's unique visual language captures the urban life scene and emotions. Infusing all their iconography with various symbols and impressions from New York streets, the duo brings New York's vibrant street language to Bodrum, this time designed in red.
