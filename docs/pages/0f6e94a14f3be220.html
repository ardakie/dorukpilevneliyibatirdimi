REFİK ANADOL'S AI DATA SCULPTURE TITLED "INDUSTRIAL DREAMS" IN HENRICHSHÜTTE HATTINGEN | PİLEVNELİ

Within the scope of the "Futur 21 - art industry culture" festival organized with the participation of 16 different industrial museums, LWL Industriemuseum in Hattingen, Germany included Refik Anadol's "Industrial Dreams". Within the scope of the festival, which aims to answer the questions about the future in historical regions, Refik Anadol's "Industrial Dreams" is exhibited on a 16-meter outdoor LED screen, hosted by LWL Industriemuseum. Visitors will be able to experience "Industrial Dreams" for the last time, which will be offered between 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. from March 12, 2022.
