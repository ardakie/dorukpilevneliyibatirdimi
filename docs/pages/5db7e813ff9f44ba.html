Refik Anadol created a special work for Akbank that explores the bank's historical journey.

The artwork is notable for being Turkey's first permanent artificial intelligence artwork. It consists of four sections: Akbank Archives, Akbank Memories, Istanbul Winds, and Coral Dreams, each reflecting both corporate history and the evolution of urban memory over time. Spanning 220 square meters and over 60 million pixels, it's one of Anadol's largest three works. Anadol expressed that it is a great honor to return to Istanbul with a work that resonates with the cultural and natural rhythm of the city.

The opening event took place on October 8, hosted by Suzan Sabancı at Akbank Headquarters.
