REFİK ANADOL STUDIO'S "SENSE OF HEALING" SALES INCOME WILL BE DONATED TO UNICEF | PİLEVNELİ

Refik Anadol Studio, which has signed one of the largest artificial intelligence data sculptures in the world, will donate the income from the sale of the work to UNICEF.
 
"Sense of Healing" will be released at UNICEF's fourth summer premiere. The artwork visualizes the recovery process of mental health patients using EEG sensors, fMRI and DTI imaging techniques.
 
“Sense of Healing”, donated to the auction by @myndcenters, a newly established company in collaboration with neuroscience pioneer @minmaze.labs and one of the leading organizations in the health industry, Therme Group @thermeartprogram, will be on sale in Capri on July 30.
