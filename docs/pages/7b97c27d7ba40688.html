"Unpromised Lands"

 PILEVNELI Project | Mecidiyeköy

February 12 - March 24, 2019

 
PILEVNELI Mecidiyeköy is preparing to host Kezban Arca Batıbeki’s solo exhibition titled “Unpromised Lands” carried out by PILEVNELI Project from February 12th to March 24th, 2019.
 
During a period of mass immigration, Batıbeki’s collages illustrate the stories of those leaving or having to leave regions that produce immigrants and reflects them with a calculated distance rather than conventionalizing or dramatizing them. The series, with Kezban Arca Batıbeki’s expressive practice, appears as a new world displaying the ordinary as well as the extraordinary conditions of the humankind.
 
Realized under the sponsorship of ASMED and Fairmont, with the support of Privee PR, Samsung, Jotun, Nespresso and Coca Cola (CCI) and with the installation support of BeetleArt.
