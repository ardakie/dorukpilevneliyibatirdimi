DEFNE TESAL'S SITE-SPECIFIC INSTALLATION AT MEŞHER | PİLEVNELİ

/news/39-defne-tesal-in-mekana-ozgu-yerlestirmesi-mesher-de/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2026-06-13
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.

One of the Pilevneli artists Defne Tesal's site-specific installation "I'm Nobody. Are You Nobody?" is meeting with its visitors in Meşher. The exhibition, which will open on September 14, 2022, is based on the unrequited love story of Echo and Narcissus in Ancient Greek mythology. The exhibition interprets the reflection, resonance and metamorphosis themes presented by this narrative in the light of today's artistic and social dynamics. Curated by Selen Ansen, the exhibition features nearly 120 works by 44 artists from Turkey and abroad. Named after Emily Dickinson's "I Am Nobody! Who are you?" Inspired by his poetry, the exhibition invites visitors to a space where relationships are woven between countless you(s), me(s), desires, narratives, images and experiences. The exhibition, which can be seen until February 12, 2023, can be visited free of charge from Tuesday to Sunday between 11:00 and 19:00.
