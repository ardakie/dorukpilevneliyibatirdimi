PİLEVNELİ is in Mexico for ZⓈONAMACO 2025!

/news/81-pilevneli-is-in-mexico-for-zonamaco-2025/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2026-02-16
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.

ZⓈONAMACO, set to take placein Mexico City, the capital of Mexico, from February 5–9, 2025, is preparing to welcome art enthusiasts for its 21st edition. As the largest and most prestigious art fair in Latin America, it brings together works from various disciplines, including contemporary and modern art, design, antiques, and photography, all under one roof.

 

With its curated sections—Main Section, Ejes, Sur, and Modern Art—the fair gathers prominent figures from the international art world while strongly reflecting the artistic diversity of Latin America.

 

PİLEVNELİ will showcase works by :mentalKLINIK, Rasim Aksan, Nevin Aladağ, Refik Anadol, Banu Anka, Lal Batman, Ali Elmacı, Ryan Gander, Kevin Francis Gray, Serkan Sarıer and Tarık Töre at booth B109 at ZⓈONAMACO.

 

For detailed artwork information, please contact us at info@pilevneli.com. For visiting hours and ticket details, please visit ZⓈONAMACO’s official website.
