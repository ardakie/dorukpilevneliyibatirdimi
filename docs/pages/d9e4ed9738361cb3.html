PİLEVNELİ AT ARTISSIMA WITH BORA AKINCITÜRK!

/news/131/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2026-06-13
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.
Bora Akıncıtürk, Untitled, 2025. Photo by Gabriele Abbruzzese

PİLEVNELİ presents Bora Akıncıtürk’s solo project “The Interior” at Artissima’s Present Future section, booth 5!

 

Invited by Joel Valabrega and Léon Kruijswijk, the curators of Artissima 2025’s Present Future, Akıncıtürk presents a selection of works in his first art fair solo exhibition that continues his exploration of dystopia, overconsumption, and artistic independence, blending pop visuals, internet-sourced images, and personal ephemera.

 

Featuring two new sound pieces and a sculptural intervention, the installation highlights the artist’s multidisciplinary practice and reflects on the industrial and ecological collapse of early 2000s capitalist optimism.

Artissima is taking place from 31 October to 2 November 2025 at OVAL, Lingotto Fiere, Turin, under this year’s theme, “Operating Manual for Spaceship Earth."
