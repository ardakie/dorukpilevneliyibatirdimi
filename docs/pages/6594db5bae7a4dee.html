Johan Creten's Wild Heart II at Sotheby's Paris | PİLEVNELİ

Johan Creten’s Wild Heart II (From the Series “Perle Noire”) is presented to collectors at Sotheby’s Paris, “Opéra de Paris : Auction for Action, Bid for Creation!” Sotheby’s and the Paris National Opera are holding the first exhibition in the history of opera, in a collaboration to support the artists who have made the opera a hub of creation for more than 350 years. The sales that will take place in this auction come to life as part of the initiative “Mon Opéra Responsible et Comgagé #MORE” reaffirming the Paris Opera’s commitment to play a greater role in society. The upcoming sales aim first and foremost to contribute to promoting the creativity that constitutes the heart of the organization's operations. "Opéra de Paris : Auction for Action, Bid for Creation!” can be attended physically on January 30th and online between January 23-31.

February 1, 2023
