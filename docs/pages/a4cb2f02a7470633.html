Event: ARTWEEKS @AKARETLER: 7TH EDITION | PİLEVNELİ

/events/12/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2026-06-10
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.
OVERVIEW

PILEVNELI is pleased to announce the participation to the 7th Edition of Artweeks @Akaretler.

 

On PILEVNELI's presentation at Artweeks @Akaretler which will take place between 1-13 November, artworks by Ali Elmacı, Arik Levy, Daniel Knorr, Banu Anka, Defne Tesal, Esra Gülmen, Frank Nitsche, Hans Op de Beeck, Mehmet & Kazım, Michael Sailstorfer, Nevin Aladağ, Refik Anadol, Serkan Sarıer, Sucuk&Bratwurst, Tarık Töre and Tim Kent will be on display.

 

In the PILEVNELI Store section, which was created as a part of the selection, the editioned merchandise of brand collaborations, PILEVNELI publications and the editioned artworks of PILEVNELI artists such as Ali Elmacı, Defne Tesal, Esra Gülmen, Mehmet & Kazım, Nevin Aladağ and Tarık Töre will also be presented to art lovers.

PILEVNELI's Artweeks@Akaretler presentation can be viewed between 1-13 November 2022 at Sıraevler No:37-39 / 1st Floor.

 

Artweeks @Akaretler PİLEVNELİ presentation is realized in cooperation with Mozaik; and with the contributions of Arzum, Atelier Rebul, illy Turkey, Jotun, Samsung Electronics Turkey and Yunus Karma.
