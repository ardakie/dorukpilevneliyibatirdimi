ZİYA TACİR, SEEKING FOR LIGHT | 3 December 2020 - 9 January 2021 | PİLEVNELİ

VIEW WORKS

"Seeking for Light"

PILEVNELI Dolapdere

December 3, 2020 – January 9, 2021

 

PILEVNELI is pleased to announce a solo exhibition titled “Seeking for Light” by photography artist Ziya Tacir between December 3, 2020 and January 9, 2021.

 

Known mostly for capturing architectural buildings in monumental scenes, the artist’s first solo show in PILEVNELI presents series of photos of Japan. Instead of his accustomed fictive works, in this recent series Tacir allows the viewer to explore the landscape and nature. Similar to Japanese aesthetics, while concentrating on delicacy and simplicity, throughout the series Tacir adopts the splendor of natural spaces.

 

Having the lyrical perspective of a 19th century photographer, Tacir changes the mindset of the viewer by presenting artistic stories of poetic and bare spaces; imbedding the photographed landscapes to our mind as if a distant memory.

 

After spending some time in isolated areas of Japan for the series, Tacir echoes the simplicity and the purity of life in his photographic works. Using simple and empty spaces, achieving clarity through silence and aloofness, the forms which the humble yet the striking allure of nature takes forces the viewer to question the passing time.

 

Ziya Tacir not only visits timeless and permanent scenes, but also revokes moments that exist for a glimpse or fade away for infinity.

 

The exhibition can be viewed at PILEVNELI Dolapdere.
