ARTIST AND DESIGNER ESRA GÜLMEN HAS BEEN INDUCTED AS THE DESIGN EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF OGILVY GERMANY | PİLEVNELİ

Artist and designer Esra Gülmen has been inducted as the design executive officer of Ogilvy Germany.


Studying human psychology and the little struggles we all face in everyday life, Gülmen will be responsible for the designs of all Ogilvy agencies in Germany, including INGO Hamburg and Social.Lab.

 

The works of the artist, represented by PILEVNELI, will meet the audience in two new exhibitions in the upcoming period. Gülmen's works will take place in the group exhibition that PILEVNELI will hold together with König Galerie in Kaplankaya during the summer season.

May 22, 2021
