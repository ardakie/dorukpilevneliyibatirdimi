ESRA GÜLMEN'S EDITIONS ON MISA ART MARKET | PİLEVNELİ

Esra Gülmen, one of the artists from PILEVNELI, will present "I will wait for you…" on Misa Art Market today. Titled editions will only be available for 24 hours.

 

In the editions art lovers have an opportunity to be a part of the artwork. The person who will own the work will be able to complete the sentence “I will wait for you..” how they feel in their own heart. If they leave it blank, Gülmen will add the last word herself. After 24 hours of sales, Gülmen will complete the editions by handwriting the last words.

 

The 24-hour drop can be accessed via www.misa.art starting from 20:00 Turkish time today.
