PİLEVNELİ IS PARTICIPATING IN ART DÜSSELDORF 2025!

/news/95-pilevneli-art-dusseldorf-2025te-yerini-aldi/ · Kayıt tarihi: 2026-06-14
Arşivden kurtarılan içerik. Sayfaların kayıt tarihleri farklıdır. İşlevler ve özgün tasarım birebir korunmamış olabilir.
Photo by GRAYSC

PILEVNELİ takes its place at the 7th edition of Art Düsseldorf, a contemporary art fair with strong ties to Germany’s rich artistic heritage!

 

Held from April 10 to 13, 2025, at Areal Böhler, the fair brings together the global art scene. At booth G12, PILEVNELİ presents works by :mentalKLINIK, Lal Batman, and Serkan Sarıer. The selection focuses on themes such as the political dynamics of coexistence, the sensory complexity of belonging, and the fluidity of identity.

 

Bringing together international art professionals, collectors, and enthusiasts every year, Art Düsseldorf once again presents a comprehensive program this year. Held in the unique atmosphere of Areal Böhler, the fair offers a dynamic and interactive platform for artists and viewers alike, where architecture adds new dimensions to the experience of art.
