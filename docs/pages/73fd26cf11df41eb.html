REFIK ANADOL AT GUGGENHEIM BILBAO! | PİLEVNELİ

Guggenheim Bilbao will host Refik Anadol’s captivating, architectural, and multisensory work from March 7 to October 19, 2025. The museum will welcome Anadol as the first artist in the in situ exhibition series. The exhibition will invite visitors to reshape their perceptions of the physical world, public art, and the boundaries of creativity.

 

2025 promises to be a significant year for Refik Anadol, not only for this exhibition but also with the opening of Dataland in Los Angeles. Located in The Grand LA, a building designed by Frank Gehry, Dataland will be the world’s first AI art museum, blending AI and data visualization technologies with art. Led by Anadol, this project will offer an interactive experience and present a new vision for the future of art.

 

Anadol’s two major projects are closely tied to Gehry’s architectural vision. By dedicating his Guggenheim Bilbao work to Gehry, Anadol honors his contributions to modern architecture while merging his artistic vision with Gehry’s spaces.
