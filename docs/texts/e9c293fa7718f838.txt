Refik Anadol's artificial intelligence data sculpture named "Winds of Dallas" took its place in AT&T Discovery District.
Located in Dallas, Texas and designed by AT&T telecommunications company as a technology, cultural and entertainment center, the work consists of images of national parks in the USA. The work, which contains the data of 110 million images, has the distinction of being the largest installation the studio has ever installed, with a height of 32 meters.
