Berlin-based artist Nevin Aladağ has been awarded the 2026 Kurt Schwitters Prize by the Lower Saxony Savings Bank Foundation.
Known for her work on themes of cultural identity and social diversity, Aladağ first gained international recognition at Documenta 14 and the Venice Biennale. Currently a professor of “Sculpture in Motion” at the Dresden University of Fine Arts, she expressed admiration for Schwitters, noting his unique blend of humor and seriousness as a major influence on her own work.
Congratulations to Aladağ on this well-deserved honor!
October 30, 2024
