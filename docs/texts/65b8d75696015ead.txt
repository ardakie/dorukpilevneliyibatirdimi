ALİ ELMACI, KAAN GÖZÜTOK, MEHMET&KAZIM, SERKAN SARIER, DEFNE TESAL, TARIK TÖRE, YUŞA YALÇINTAŞ
WORKS ON PAPER
22.12 - 27.01
PİLEVNELİ | DOLAPDERE
PİLEVNELİ, is pleased to announce the new group exhibition of Ali Elmacı, Kaan Gözütok, Mehmet&Kazım, Serkan Sarıer, Defne Tesal, Tarık Töre, and Yuşa Yalçıntaş. The exhibition scheduled to take place between December 22 - January 27 will showcase the artists' work on paper.
