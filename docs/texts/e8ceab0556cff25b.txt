One of the world's largest artificial intelligence data sculptures, "Sense of Healing", signed by Refik Anadol Studio, went up for auction at UNICEF's fourth summer gala.
Visualizing the recovery processes of mental health patients using EEG sensors, fMRI and DTI imaging techniques, the work has reached 1,700,000.-Euro at the auction held in Capri this evening.
**Proceeds from the "Sense of Healing" sale organized by the newly established @myndcenters in cooperation with @minmaze.labs, a pioneer in neuroscience, and Therme Group @thermeartprogram, one of the leading organizations in the health industry, will be donated to UNICEF.
July 31, 2022
