PİLEVNELİ will present Lal Batman’s solo exhibition
"The Grand Excess"
at its Dolapdere space  between January 29 and March 14, 2026.
In
"The Grand Excess"
, Lal Batman questions the glittering face of social media while confronting  the elegance of the past with the artificial grandeur of the present. Historical layers spanning  Ancient Egypt and Greece to the 18th, 19th, and 21st centuries converge with aesthetic  traditions from South America, the Far East, the Middle East, and the West. This convergence is  not a mere coincidence; it stands as a profound and multi-layered critique of the superficiality  that defines our era.
Batman’s works come to life on carved, textured paper, where digital and plastic elements meet  ink, acrylic, natural and glass stones, and pearls. The interplay of these diverse materials offers  viewers an intense sensory experience, transforming fragments of historical memory into vivid,  tactile components of the present.
"The Grand Excess" emerges as a visual manifesto that places the aesthetic values of the past  within a contemporary context. Emphasizing that differences can not only coexist but also enrich  one another, the exhibition redefines grandeur—not as mere spectacle, but as a timeless symbol  of cultural dialogue, resilience, and harmony.
One of the notable elements of the exhibition is an original perfume designed and developed by  MG Gülçiçek to reflect the artist’s story and energy, offering visitors a multi sensory olfactory  experience. Accompanying the artworks throughout the exhibition space, this scent deepens the  audience’s engagement and highlights the multidimensional nature of art.
