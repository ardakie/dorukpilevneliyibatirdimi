İ
LEVNEL

Yalıkavak. In
Mural 3000
, Mehmet & Kazım magnifies the characteristic Edding drawings impressively which are reminiscent of "throw-ups," a graffiti style that has shaped metropolises for over 50 years. Inspired by Francisco G
oya's Pinturas Negras
, abstract expressionists from the 1950s, Paul Klee's
Geschwister
, Pierre Soulages' works, and modern scenes from Brooklyn's Bushwick, Mehmet & Kazım's unique visual language captures the urban life scene and emotions. Infusing all their iconography with various symbols and impressions from New York streets, the duo brings New York's vibrant street language to Bodrum, this time designed in red.
