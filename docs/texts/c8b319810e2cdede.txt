Johan Creten’s solo exhibition “Far removed from the numbing speed” will be on view from November 14 to December 20, 2024 at Almina Rech, London.
The artwork “La Mouche Morte” masterfully disrupts the chaos of modern life, inviting us into a space of renewal and reflection.
Congratulations to Johan Creten for his solo exhibition!
