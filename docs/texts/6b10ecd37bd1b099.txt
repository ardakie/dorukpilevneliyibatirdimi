Daniel Firman's new exhibition titled “An object never comes alone” is at Ceysson Benetiere Lyon. In the exhibition, previously unexhibited works of Firman from the “Mini-gathering” series will be presented.
Works from the "Gathering" series, which he started to produce for the first time in the early 2000s, were presented to art lovers in PİLEVNELİ Mecidiyeköy at the "10 Artists / 10 Individual Practices" exhibition in 2018. The series, which the artist developed and changed over time, was shown at the "Plastic Confetti" exhibition held in Ceysson Benetiere New York in 2019, and then at the "Wall Dancing" exhibition in PİLEVNELİ Mandarin Oriental, Bodrum in the summer of 2021.
Opening today, “An object never comes alone” will welcome visitors to Lyon until 17 December 2022.
