The artistic productions of Sucuk & Bratwurst, an artist collective and creative studio founded in 2014 by Denis Olgac, Lukas Olgac, Alessandro Belliero and David Gönner, mainly consist of moving and fixed three-dimensional and digital works. The four of them met in their childhood and started their creative work with the blog they founded in 2012. Sharing photographs, collages, traditional drawings and three-dimensional works on their blogs, the group began receiving invitations to solo and group exhibitions. With the establishment of the creative agency in 2014, they started to produce both liberal arts and design and commercial works. Sucuk & Bratwurst, whose commercial projects include Alexander Wang, Rimowa, Moncler, Nike, Adidas, Porsche, Converse, Dior and Kenzo, also worked with musicians such as Lena Meyer-Landrut, Post Malone and Dua Lipa.
