PILEVNELI is pleased to announce the representation of German artist Michael Sailstorfer!
Born in Velden, Germany in 1979, Michael Sailstorfer completed his art education at the Munich Fine Arts Academy and pursued his master degree at Goldsmiths University of London and graduated in 2004.
The artist lives and works in Berlin.
Works by Michael Sailstorfer are in the collections of many prestigious art institutions such as Center Pompidou (France), Fondazione Morra Greco (Italy), Jerry Speyer (USA), Leopold Hoesch Museum (Germany), Mariano Pichler Collection (Italy), MARTa Herford (Germany), MUSEION (Italy), Museum Kurhaus Kleve (Germany), Paolo Kind (England), Sammlung Boros (Germany), Sammlung Goetz (Germany), Städel Museum (Germany) and Walker Art Center (USA).
