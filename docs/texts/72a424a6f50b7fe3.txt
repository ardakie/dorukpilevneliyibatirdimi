Daniel Knorr’s site-specific installation
“Calligraphic Wig”
will be on view at
Odunpazarı Modern Museum in Eskişehir
from
October 25, 2025, to March 21, 2026
. A product of the artist’s time spent in plastic recycling factories in Hong Kong,
Calligraphic Wig
transforms the spontaneous forms that emerge when machines malfunction into subjects of art.
By turning the shapes born from the production and transformation processes of plastic into an artistic language,
Calligraphic Wig
invites viewers to confront the material’s potential. These accidental plastic formations sometimes resemble the letters of an unknown alphabet, sometimes deep-sea creatures, and at other times organic structures from other planets.
First exhibited at
Chungking Mansions
, the work draws inspiration from
Hong Kong’s cultural diversity and urban aesthetics
. Knorr coats some pieces in
automobile paint colors ranging from the 1970s to the present
, reestablishing the connection between industrial production and aesthetics, while leaving others in their raw state to highlight the material’s primal energy and accidental transformation.
More than a visual experience,
Calligraphic Wig
is also a
call for environmental awareness
and an exploration of
a new aesthetic language born from discarded materials
.
October 28, 2025
