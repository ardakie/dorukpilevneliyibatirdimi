PİLEVNELİ is pleased to announce its representation of Lal Batman!
Born in 2001 in Bursa, Lal Batman explores how people perceive art by blending figurative and conceptual styles. Her works focus on the interaction between space, the viewer, and the artwork, reflecting the transition from traditional to intellectual art. Having participated in international projects across Europe, Asia, South America, and the Balkans, the artist invites audiences to experience her art on a deeper level. Using diverse materials, she creates intricate compositions that push the boundaries of traditional art.
