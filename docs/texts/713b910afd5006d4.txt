PILEVNELI is pleased to announce the participation to the 7th Edition of Artweeks @Akaretler.
On PILEVNELI's presentation at Artweeks @Akaretler which will take place between 1-10 November, Ali Elmacı, Arik Levy, Daniel Knorr, Banu Anka, Defne Tesal, Esra Gülmen, Frank Nitsche, Hans Op de Beeck, Mehmet & Kazım, Michael Sailstorfer, Nevin Aladağ, Works by Refik Anadol, Serkan Sarıer, Sucuk&Bratwurst, Tarık Töre and Tim Kent will be on display.
In the PILEVNELI Store section, which was created as a part of the selection, the editioned merchandise of brand collaborations, PILEVNELI publications and the editioned artworks of PILEVNELI artists such as Ali Elmacı, Defne Tesal, Esra Gülmen, Mehmet & Kazım, Nevin Aladağ and Tarık Töre will also be presented to art lovers.
PILEVNELI's Artweeks@Akaretler presentation can be viewed between 1-10 November 2022 at Sıraevler No:37-39 / 1st Floor.
October 14, 2022
