Johan Creten, considered among the pioneering artists who had ceramics accepted as a contemporary art form, will be showcasing his work alongside Japanese artist Izumi Kato at Art Collaboration 2024 in Kyoto. The fair, taking place from November 1-3, serves as a bridge between Japanese and international galleries, government and private sectors, and fine arts and other cultural spheres. The collaboration between Creten and Kato establishes a connection between primal expression and fascination.
Congratulations to Creten for his achievements!
November 1, 2024
