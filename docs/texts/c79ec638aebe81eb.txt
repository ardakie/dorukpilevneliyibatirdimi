The exhibition titled "Let's Get Digital", includes works by artists such as Beeple, Anyma and Daniel Arsham and covers the NFT revolution at Palazzo Strozzi under many titles. “Let’s Get Digital” focuses on many topics, from digital installations to multimedia experiences, from the versatility of crypto art to digital certificates that completely change the concepts of originality and singularity of an artwork. Visitors are invited to witness the development and evolution where technology has changed even the aesthetic perceptions.
Curated by Arturo Galansino and Serena Tabacchi, the exhibition opened on 18th May and will continue until 31st July, 2022. It is announced to those who find their ways at Florence.
