PİLEVNELİ is at the 17th edition of Art Dubai in both Contemporary and Digital sections.
PİLEVNELİ is participating in the Contemporary section with Nevin Aladağ, Banu Anka, Johan Creten, Ali Elmacı, Esra Gülmen, Daniel Knorr, Arik Levy, Mehmet & Kazım, Hans Op de Beeck, Michael Sailstorfer, Serkan Sarıer, Defne Tesal, Tarık Töre and Yuşa Yalçıntaş
.
In the Digital section, a selection of works by Refik Anadol, Ali Elmacı and Hans Op de Beeck will be presented.
PİLEVNELİ can be visited between 1-5 March 2023 at booth F9 in the Contemporary section and X18 in the Digital section of Art Dubai.
March 17, 2023
