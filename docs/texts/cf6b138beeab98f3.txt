PİLEVNELİ has been welcoming visitors to its Dolapdere venue with
Viewing Room
since November 26. This special compilation features works from the gallery’s inventory, including both previously exhibited pieces and others that have never been shown to the public before.
Throughout December, the exhibition invites visitors to experience a gallery concept that goes beyond the traditional confines of an exhibition space, utilizing the spatial possibilities of PİLEVNELİ Dolapdere. The works are displayed across all levels of the gallery using a hybrid approach that combines traditional exhibition methods with storage-inspired arrangements.
Viewing Room
decodes the relationship between art, time, and space from an unorthodox lens. You can visit the exhibition at PİLEVNELİ Dolapdere until December 28.
In addition, we would be delighted to welcome you to join us in celebrating our exhibition during a special showcase on Saturday, December 7, from 12:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., accompanied by aperitifs.
For further information, please contact us at info@pilevneli.com.
