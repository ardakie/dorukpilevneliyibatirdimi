The 16th Lyon Biennial, which opened its doors on September 14, 2022 with the theme of "Manifesto of Fragility" this year, will continue to host art lovers until December 31, 2022.
Hans Op de Beeck, one of the 87 artists of the biennial, has created a custom installation of 1900 square meters located in the Fagor Factory. In his work titled “We were the last to stay”, Op de Beeck brings to life a campsite where nomadic families lived and abandoned, and tells a story with every caravan in the campsite.
