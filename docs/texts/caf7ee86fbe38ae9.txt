PİLEVNELİ is taking part in the 5th edition of Enter Art Fair in Copenhagen.
PİLEVNELİ, which presents a selection of Tarık Töre’s works at Enter Art Fair, can be visited at booth number 69 between August 24-27, 2023. Enter Art Fair, one of the largest international art fairs in Northern Europe, is held in Copenhagen and brings together 88 of the world’s leading galleries from 22 countries and 36 cities.
The 5th edition of Enter Art Fair takes place in Lokomotivværkstedet, one of Copenhagen’s liveliest cultural venues.
The 10,000 m2 historical venue was used for the maintenance of Danish locomotives and is known for its industrial architecture and symbolic green color.
