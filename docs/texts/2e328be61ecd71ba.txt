PİLEVNELİ will take part in the 20th edition of Contemporary Istanbul, held at Tersane Istanbul from September 24 to 28, 2025. For this milestone edition, the gallery will present a specially curated selection at its Hall A Loft space (located opposite the main entrance of Rixos Tersane Istanbul), featuring works by :mentalKLINIK, Rasim Aksan, Nevin Aladağ, Banu Anka, Lal Batman, Johan Creten, Ali Elmacı, Kevin Francis Gray, Daniel Knorr, Tony Matelli, and Pınar Yolaçan.
The presentation, composed of works by artists represented by the gallery, will be on view in a dedicated space at the fair’s entrance. As part of the “Extended Format” initiative developed by Contemporary Istanbul and Tersane Istanbul, this selection will continue to be accessible through different displays at Tersane Istanbul until November.
With the supports of: Borusan Otomotiv, Atelier Rebul, Lazzoni Hotel, Uludağ Premium, Jotun Türkiye.
