The exhibition titled “Nature Dreams” will host art lovers in the “Art Axis” section, one of the largest exhibition areas in ARKEN, as of February 10, 2023. Out of the three works in the exhibition, one is designed specifically for the site— meteorological data from around the museum will simultaneously be reflected on the exterior of the building. Refik Anadol's exhibition titled "Nature Dreams" will be open to visit at ARKEN Museum for Modern Art, Denmark from February 10 to August 27, 2023.
