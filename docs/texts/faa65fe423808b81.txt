OVERVIEW
PİLEVNELİ takes part in Art Show: Galeriler Buluşması 2026 at Booth B16
This year,
PİLEVNELİ
welcomes visitors to
Art Show: Galeriler Buluşması 2026
with a curated selection of works by Irish artist
Kevin Francis Gray
and German-born artist
Frank Nitsche
.
Bringing together Kevin Francis Gray’s dynamic sculptural compositions and Frank Nitsche’s works shaped by Constructivist influences and contemporary design aesthetics, PİLEVNELİ aims to establish a strong dialogue between different disciplines of contemporary art.
Conceived by the
Association of International Art Galleries (USGD)
to contribute to the transformation of contemporary art,
Art Show: Galeriler Buluşması 2026
will take place from
12–15 February 2026
at
2Plan Terminal – Etiler
, with
Yapı Kredi Private Banking
as the main sponsor. Centered on solidarity rather than competition, the fair brings together
28 galleries
, offering a transparent, accessible, and non-hierarchical platform for the art world.
