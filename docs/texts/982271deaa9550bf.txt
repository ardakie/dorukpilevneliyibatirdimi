Contemporary art fair, which will be held in its 14th edition, gathers selected galleries that will exhibit digital works and NFT under the name of “Art Dubai Digital” for the first time this year in a separate section.
Art Dubai aims to unite the crypto and art world in Dubai, which is on its way to becoming one of the international crypto hubs.
PILEVNELI will bring together a selection of Refik Anadol's works with art lovers at the "Art Dubai Digital" booth. After the VIP preview that will take place on March 8, 2022, the fair will continue with VIP days on March 9-10, 2022. It will be open to all visitors between 11 - 13 March 2022. It is announced to those who pass by Dubai.
