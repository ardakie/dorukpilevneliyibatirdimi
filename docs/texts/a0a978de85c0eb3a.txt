PILEVNELI is pleased to announce the first solo exhibition in the gallery space by Defne Tesal known for her drawings, embroideries and site specific installations. The exhibition titled ‘’Hesitation’’ will take place for the first time in PILEVNELI between May 25 - June 29, 2021. The exhibition will also consist a site specific installation focusing on the sensory and emotional movements within hesitation.
