The premiere of
Inner Portrait
, a collaboration between Refik Anadol and Turkish Airlines, took place on November 27 at the Atatürk Cultural Center (AKM) in Istanbul. The project focuses on the experiences of four individuals from different continents who have never traveled before. During the launch event, a documentary inspired by the artwork was screened for the first time, exploring the impact of travel on human biology.
Inner Portrait
will be open to visitors at the Atatürk Cultural Center until December 1.
