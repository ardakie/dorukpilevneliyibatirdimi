PİLEVNELİ is excited to welcome you to the 19th edition of Contemporary Istanbul, taking place at Tersane Istanbul from October 23 to 27, 2024!
As one of Turkey’s major contemporary art fairs, Contemporary Istanbul will showcase its 19th edition at Tersane Istanbul. PİLEVNELİ, located at booth B1-08, will exhibit a selection of works by :mentalKLINIK, Bora Akıncıtürk, Rasim Aksan, Nevin Aladağ, Adrian Altıntaş, Refik Anadol, Banu Anka, Johan Creten, Ali Elmacı, Mehmet & Kazım, Daniel Knorr, Arik Levy, Frank Nitsche, Serkan Sarıer, Tarık Töre, Yuşa Yalçıntaş and Pınar Yolaçan.
Contemporary Istanbul PİLEVNELI presentation is realized in cooperation with Mozaik.
October 18, 2024
