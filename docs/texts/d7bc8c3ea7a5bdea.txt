PILEVNELI
is pleased to announce the first exhibition in Turkey of world-renowned British artist Richard Wilson on September 1, 2021.
Known for his works interfering with the architectural space and inspired by the disciplines of construction and engineering, Wilson’s solo exhibition entitled “Stealing Space” presents the artist’s sculptures and drawings as well as the iconic installation “20:50”.
