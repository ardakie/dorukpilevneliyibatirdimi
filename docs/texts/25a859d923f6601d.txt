"ANADOL - CRETEN - ELMACI - FIRMAN - TÖRE - ZÜMRÜTOĞLU"
PILEVNELI | Mandarin Oriental, Bodrum
10 June - 4 July 2021
The group exhibition titled
“ANADOL - CRETEN - ELMACI - FIRMAN - TÖRE - ZÜMRÜTOĞLU”
can be visited from June 10th to July 4th, 2021. The selection presented includes Refik Anadol’s ISS data paintings, Daniel Firman’s sculptures from “Gathering” and “Attitude” series, recent works of Ali Elmacı, Tarık Töre and paintings from  Erdoğan Zümrütoğlu’s “Summer Days Don’t Remember Me” and “Yağ Satarım, Bal Satarım, Ustam Ölmüş Ben Satarım” series. Johan Creten’s iconic “Observation Points” will offer the visitors a chance to sit down, take a moment and observe the artworks.
The exhibition at
PILEVNELI
Mandarin Oriental Bodrum is held in collaboration with ŠKODA, Samsung and MG International Fragrance Company.
