Refik Anadol's "Casa Batlló: Living Architecture" will be auctioned at Christie's New York "21st Century Evening Sale" on May 10.
“Casa Batlló: Living Architecture” reinterprets the real-time data of the building's façade. The dynamic NFT is not only the only digital artwork offered at Christie's 21st Century Evening Sale, but also pioneers in transforming a UNESCO world heritage site into NFT format for the first time. The work will be open to bidding with an estimate of 1,000,000-2,000,000 US dollars.
