Nevin Aladağ's first solo exhibition to be held at Galerie Krinzinger opens its doors with a preview to be held on 10 November 2022.
In the exhibition titled "Tuning", works from the series "Social Fabric", "Jali" and "Resonating Space" from the artist's last exhibition held in PİLEVNELİ will be presented. In addition, the 'Stilletto' series, which was inspired by the artist's 'Raise the Roof' performance shown at the Venice Biennale in 2017, and the 'Tusch-carneval' installation, which creates a musical note section on the wall from cannonballs from the 17th century, will also be exhibited in the “Tuning” exhibition.
The “Tuning” exhibition will host art lovers who are in Vienna between 10 November and 10 December 2022.
