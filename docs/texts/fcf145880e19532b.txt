PILEVNELI Yalıkavak Opens on July 2nd
PILEVNELI
is pleased to announce its new venue, which will open on Saturday, July 2, 2022.
PILEVNELI Yalıkavak
will host visitors until September 18, 2022.
PILEVNELI Yalıkavak
is located on a 6.5-acre land consisting of 1500 square meters of enclosed and 5000 square meters of open space. The location is made up of five separate levels of which, two are reserved as gallery spaces. In addition to the exhibition areas, Zaaf restaurant will offer visitors a gastronomic experience. Additionally, books published by
PILEVNELI
will be showcased in the
PILEVNELI
Store.
PILEVNELI
artists Esra Gülmen, Ali Elmacı, Tarık Töre, and Mehmet & Kazım's collaboration with Anim, Les Benjamins, Mergim, Misela, and Monsieur Chaussette brands will be on sale at the store in
PILEVNELI Yalıkavak
throughout the summer. Between June 2-17, Midnight Express will have its pop-up store located next to the gallery spaces.
PILEVNELI Yalıkavak
, with its unique scenery, will create a contemporary art space in Bodrum that offers a riveting experience to its visitors this summer.
PILEVNELI Yalıkavak
welcomes art lovers with two different exhibitions.
Group exhibition consisting of the latest productions of PILEVNELI artists in Yalıkavak
The first exhibition opening in
PILEVNELI Yalıkavak
, will extend to various locations within the project, including all of the stone houses and gardens surrounding the buildings. Bora Akıncıtürk, Nevin Aladağ, Refik Anadol, Nobuyoshi Araki, Stephan Balkenhol, Johan Creten, Ali Elmacı, Jan Fabre, Esra Gülmen, Hechizoo, Tim Kent, Daniel Knorr, Arik Levy, Bjarne Melgaard, Mehmet & Kazım, Frank Nitsche, Hans Works by Op de Beeck, Zoe Ouvrier, Michael Sailstorfer, Serkan Sarıer, Meltem Sarıkaya, Defne Tesal, Tarık Töre, Richard Wilson and Erdoğan Zümrütoğlu will meet art lovers in Bodrum.
