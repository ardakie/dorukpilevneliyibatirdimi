Refik Anadol's work, which was exhibited in Milan's Duomo Square in October last year and inspired by the "Serpenti" collection of the Italian jewelery brand Bulgari, is exhibited at the Saatchi Gallery in London.
The spiral data installation titled “Metamorphosis: Serpenti” is the world's first multi-sensory data sculpture using real-time artificial intelligence and scent generation. The exhibition, which opened with a special invitation last night, will host visitors until 23 December 2022.
