PİLEVNELİ is delighted to announce the opening of DATALAND, realized by Refik Anadol in Los Angeles.
Conceived as the world’s first Museum of AI Arts, DATALAND redefines the boundaries of creative collaboration between humans and machines, emerging from the artist and his team’s long-standing practice. Shaped through nature-inspired data worlds, the experience invites viewers into an immersive and multi-layered field of perception.
Extending Refik Anadol’s pioneering approach into a new dimension, the museum offers a compelling proposition on the evolving modes of production and experience in contemporary art.
DATALAND will be open to visitors in Los Angeles as of June 20.
