:mentalKLINIK is participating in the
HOME ALMOST HOME
group exhibition, curated by Joël Andrianomearisoa, at Flow Gallery in Madagascar.
The exhibition, offering a fresh interpretation of Joël Andrianomearisoa’s globally recognized concept, is being held in Antananarivo, the capital of Madagascar, following previous shows in Paris, Chicago, and Madrid.
HOME ALMOST HOME
explores the idea of home not just as a physical space but as a place where emotions and memories converge.
While reflecting an unconventional approach to the concept of “home” the exhibition is enriched by contributions from internationally recognized artists like :mentalKLINIK. The bold visual language of :mentalKLINIK stands out, while the dialogue with works by other artists transforms the exhibition into a multi-layered experience.
The collaboration between Andrianomearisoa and :mentalKLINIK invites art lovers to transcend time and space. Providing an unforgettable contribution to Madagascar’s contemporary art scene,
HOME ALMOST HOME
will be on display at Flow Gallery from November 22, 2024, to January 21, 2025.
