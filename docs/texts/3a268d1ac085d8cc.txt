One of the artists of PILEVNELI, Defne Tesal's installation meets the audience in Arter. The works in the group exhibition "Koyun Koyuna", which will be previewed on May 18, center around the themes of sleep related with privacy and sharing, individuality and community, embracement and limitation, resistance and surrender, progress and repetition. Curated by Eda Berkmen, the exhibition "Koyun Koyuna", including Defne Tesal's impressive site-specific installation, can be visited until 23 January 2023.
