The works of Johan Creten are in Paris Dior, redesigned by Peter Marino!
Marino recreated the 2,000 square meter space, seeing it as a kind of theater stage or a set where many different plays can be performed. The space was designed with an interconnected series created with open spaces, design styles and inspirations from different periods. The gardens and interiors used by all parts of the brand include modern elements and materials, while at the same time, we are faced with a restored façade and classical wooden sills that refer to Dior's history. Furnitures of many famous designers such as Joaquim Tenreiro, Hans Olsen and Gio Ponti are accompanied by the works of artists such as Johan Creten, Jennifer Steinkamp, Guy Limone and Ina Genzken.
April 16, 2022
