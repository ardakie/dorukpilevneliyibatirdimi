Rasim Aksan's work will be featured in the Charity Auction held as part of Gala Modern, organized by Istanbul Modern every December. The purpose of the auction is to channel the proceeds into the development of the museum's exhibition and education programs. It aims to contribute to the creation of a sustainable cultural legacy for future generations, as Turkey's first modern and contemporary art museum.
Artwork Details:
Untitled, 2025
Colored pencil and acrylic airbrush on paper
150 x 80 cm
This year, Gala Modern will take place on Saturday, December 13, 2025.
