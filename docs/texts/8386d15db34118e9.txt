TERSANE ISTANBUL
A1-103
PILEVNELI is pleased to announce that will participate in the 16th edition of Contemporary Istanbul with KÖNIG GALERIE.
In the selection consisting of artists represented by PILEVNELI and KÖNIG GALERIE, Bora Akıncıtürk, Nevin Aladağ, Refik Anadol, Nobuyoshi Araki, Daniel Arsham, Claudia Comte, Johan Creten, Ali Elmacı, Efsun Erkılıç, Jan Fabre, Daniel Firman, Esra Gülmen, Katharina Grosse, Jeppe Hein, Sheila Hicks, Annette Kelm, Tim Kent, Daniel Knorr, Arik Levy, Kris Martin, Mehmet + Kazım, Bjarne Melgaard, Sarah Morris, Hans Op de Beeck, Şener Özmen, Anselm Reyle, Serkan Sarıer, Jeremy Shaw, Defne Tesal , Tarık Töre, Matthias Weischer, Erwin Wurm and Erdoğan Zümrütoğlu.
The fair, which will be hosted at Tersane Istanbul for the first time this year, will be seen by visitors on October 7, 2021, after the preview on October 5-6, 2021.
You can visit PILEVNELI | KÖNIG GALERIE at booth A1-103 until October 10,  2021.
