Refik Anadol's exhibition titled “Machine Memoirs: Space", held in PILEVNELI between March-April 2021, was awarded the iF Design Award.
The artist's most comprehensive exhibition to date, held in Istanbul, took place on five floors of the gallery. Despite the pandemic, nearly 40,000 people visited the exhibition.
