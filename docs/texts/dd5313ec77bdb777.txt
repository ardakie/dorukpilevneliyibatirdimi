"AT THE FACTORY: 10 ARTISTS, 10 INDIVIDUAL PRACTICES"
PILEVNELI Mecidiyeköy
December 11, 2018 - January 27, 2019
The opening of
PILEVNELI
Mecidiyeköy's first exhibition,
"AT THE FACTORY: 10 ARTISTS, 10 INDIVIDUAL PRACTICES"
will be held on December 11, 2018 from 6:00 to 9:00 pm. The group exhibition by 10 of PİLEVNELİ resident artists, will carry the works of Refik Anadol, Hans Op de Beeck, Daniel Firman, Arik Levy, Tony Matelli, Ida Tursic&Wilfried Mille, Youssef Nabil, Şener Özmen, Jean Pigozzi and Erdoğan Zümrütoğlu. As part of the opening, Daniel Firman, Arik Levy, Tony Matelli and Refik Anadol will hold artist talks of which the schedule is below. We will be honored to have you among us at the
PILEVNELI
Mecidiyeköy opening. The exhibition is sponsored by ASMED and Fairmont, and with the support of Samsung, Jotun, Nespresso, Coca Cola (CCI) and Uludağ.
Artist Talk Schedule:
19:00 - 19:10 : Tony Matelli (Salon 6)

19:40 - 19:50 : Daniel Firman (Salon 10)
